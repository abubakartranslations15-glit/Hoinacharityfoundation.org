About HOINA

Built by someone who refused to look away

HOINA is not a foreign idea parachuted into Adamawa State. It is a daughter of this land, built by someone who knows its pain — and believes, with every fibre of her legal training, that it deserves better.

Halima UsmanBSc Law · Founder & CEO
Meet the founder

Halima Usman

"I did not build HOINA because I had all the answers. I built it because I could no longer sit with the questions — not while children were losing their futures and families were losing their dignity."

There are people who see injustice and turn away, and there are those who see it and cannot sleep. Halima has not slept easily since childhood — not because life was unkind to her, but because she was paying attention. She watched the children who never returned to class. She listened to the mothers who had nowhere to turn. She read the laws that promised protection to people who had never once heard their own rights spoken aloud. That attention became a law degree, and that law degree became HOINA.

Her journey to HOINA

From one girl's education to a foundation's mission

01

The Awakening

Growing up in Adamawa State, Halima witnessed firsthand what poverty does to a child's future — a neighbour's daughter pulled from school at nine, a widow left without recourse, a boy beaten by his employer with no one to speak for him. These were not distant statistics. They were faces she knew.

02

The Education

Driven by the belief that the law should serve everyone — not only those who can afford it — Halima pursued a BSc in Law. Every lecture strengthened her resolve to become the bridge between rights written in ink and the communities that needed them most.

03

The Foundation

HOINA was born not from a boardroom or a grant proposal — it was born from a mother's tears, a child's empty schoolbag, and Halima's refusal to walk away. She gathered a small circle of committed young people and went to work.

04

The Mission Today

HOINA has returned over 1,100 children to school and supported families across 18+ communities. For Halima, every number is a name. She continues to lead with the same conviction that started it all.

Our mission

A world where every child's future is decided by their potential, not their postcode

We work directly in the communities of Adamawa State to protect children's right to education, respond to gender-based violence, and stand beside families in crisis — with dignity, not charity as spectacle.

Our vision

Communities that no longer need us, because they have what they need to thrive

We measure success not by how long a family depends on HOINA, but by how quickly they no longer have to.

What guides us

Our values

Dignity first

We serve people, not statistics — every interaction protects someone's self-respect.

We show up

Our team sits beside the people we serve, in every room that matters.

Inclusion, always

Every program is open to everyone, regardless of gender, religion, tribe or ability.

Accountability

100% of public donations go directly toward the communities we serve.

Our team

A small, growing team of volunteers

HOINA is still young as a registered foundation — led by a core team, and powered by volunteers who show up because the mission matters to them.
