Why we exist

Some children lose their future quietly. We refuse to let that happen unnoticed.

HOINA was built by people who grew up watching it happen — a classmate pulled out of school, a family with no one to turn to, a right written into law but never reaching the people it was meant for. We close that gap directly, in the communities where it opens.
